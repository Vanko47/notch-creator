Privacy Policy

Notch Creator (Android app, package com.notchcreator.app) · Last updated 28 October 2026

In short: Notch Creator does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. Everything you create stays on your phone.

Who we are

Notch Creator is made by Vanko47 Labs. Contact: vanko47labs@gmail.com.

Data the app does not collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your phone to us or to anyone else. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools in the app.

Camera and microphone

  • Camera is used only to record the videos you choose to make.
  • Microphone is used to record your video's sound and, when "Follow my voice" is on, to move the script along as you speak. The sound is analysed on your phone, in real time, and is not sent anywhere.
  • On Android 13 and newer, the app may use Android's on-device speech recognition to match your words to the script. The app asks only for on-device recognition, which runs on your phone.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone's settings.

Your scripts and videos

  • Scripts, settings and unexported drafts are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Exported videos are saved to your phone's gallery (Movies/NotchCreator).
  • Nothing is uploaded by the app. If you share a video, you choose the app it goes to, and that app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its private data (scripts, settings and drafts). Exported videos stay in your gallery until you delete them.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include the app's scripts and settings in that backup. This is handled by Android and Google under your own account and settings; we never receive it. Drafts (videos) are excluded from backups.

Floating prompter and notifications

"Display over other apps" and notifications are requested only if you use the floating prompter. They are used to show your script over other apps and to keep it running; no information is collected.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
